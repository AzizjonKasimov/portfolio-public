# Woosong University Graduation Awards

Two awards given at the 2023학년도 후기 학위수여식 (graduation ceremony) on 2024-08-23, the same day
as the diploma. The ceremony program in
[../education/certificates/](../education/certificates/) lists Kasimov Azizjon under both. Both
certificates name the major as 미래기술학부 데이터사이언스전공 (School of Future Technology, Data
Science major).

## Academic Excellence Award (학업우수상)

Scan: [certificates/woosong-academic-excellence-award.pdf](certificates/woosong-academic-excellence-award.pdf)

- Certificate 제2024-758호, dated 2024-08-23, from the President of Woosong University (오덕성).
- Reason on the certificate: excellent grades and exemplary conduct during studies, a model for
  others (재학중 학업성적이 우수하고 품행이 방정하여 타의 모범이 되므로).
- The ceremony program lists it as a 총장상 (President's award).

## Commendation Award (공로상)

Scan: [certificates/woosong-commendation-award.pdf](certificates/woosong-commendation-award.pdf)

- Certificate 제2024-737호, dated 2024-08-23, from the President of Woosong University (오덕성).
- Reason on the certificate: diligent, responsible work in assigned duties that contributed to the
  university's development (재학중 근면 성실한 자세와 투철한 책임감으로 맡은 바 직무를 수행하여
  학교발전에 기여한 공로가 크므로).
- "Commendation Award" is the English name used on LinkedIn; 공로상 is also often translated as
  Merit Award.

## Public Use

The portfolio website shows both certificates and lists the two awards together: "Given at
graduation for grades, conduct, and service to the university."
