# LINC 3.0 Program Award

Received the "실용상" (Practical Application Award) as a member of team BLUE SKY in the
LINC 3.0 Smart City Challenge in Daejeon, South Korea.

The team project focused on predicting odor levels in water treatment plants using multivariate
time-series data, machine learning, deep learning, and specialized preprocessing techniques.

LinkedIn notes certificate number 2023-1033 and an issue date of 2023-12-27.
