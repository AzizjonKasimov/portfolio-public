# Finalist - Silicon Valley Business Plan Competition Award

Led an international team of five to the finals of the Silicon Valley Business Plan Competition
with "Blah Blah Talk," an AI language-learning MVP.

The concept included AI-powered character animation and mouth movement, natural-language
conversation practice, cross-cultural team leadership, and a complete business plan and pitch.

Successfully presented the business concept to industry experts and investors and advanced to
the final round of the competition.
