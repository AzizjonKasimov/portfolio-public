# Daejeon Global Tech Coffee

English-friendly casual tech coffee meetup organized in Daejeon to help people in tech, AI, and
data meet locally and exchange opportunities.

## Scale (from Azizjon's meetup posts, September 2026)

- 12 meetups by 2026-09-25, and meetup #13 on 2026-09-27 (see the Facebook page below).
- Recent meetups drew 12 to 13 people, including two founders who took the train from Seoul to
  see whether Daejeon has a tech community.
- Hosted in a free room provided by the city, two minutes from the subway.
- Azizjon started it because meeting people in tech in Daejeon as a foreigner was hard. The one
  rule in the intro round: say what you are stuck on right now.

LinkedIn lists the role under Volunteering as Founder and Organizer (since 2026-09-25), and
LinkedIn Featured shows the 12th-meetup post (`profile/linkedin.md`). Since 2026-10-05 the LinkedIn
About and Volunteering text say "13 meetups so far".
