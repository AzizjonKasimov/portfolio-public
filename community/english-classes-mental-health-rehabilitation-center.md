# English Classes at 좋은이웃센터

Azizjon volunteers at 좋은이웃센터, a psychiatric rehabilitation facility (정신재활시설) in
Daeheung-dong, Jung-gu, Daejeon (as a SmartToday news article describes it). Once a week he
teaches a one-hour elementary English class to the people in its program. He started in September
2026 (his recollection) and had taught four classes by 2026-10-05.

Not recorded: how he got involved.
