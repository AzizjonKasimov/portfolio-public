# Automatic Music Generator

**April 2022 - June 2022 | Woosong University**

Deep-learning project using an LSTM-based model to process `.wav` audio files and generate new
`.wav` files. LinkedIn describes the workflow as dividing audio files into notes, converting
notes to pitch, and generating a new audio track after training on the dataset.
