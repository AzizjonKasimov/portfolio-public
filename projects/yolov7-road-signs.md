# Early Road Sign Detection Practice Using YOLOv7

**September 2022 - December 2022 | Woosong University | Python, Roboflow**

This was a small early-stage learning project completed during university. It is retained only
as a historical project record and is not used as primary evidence in current resumes or
applications.

- Labeled a small custom road-sign dataset and experimented with data augmentation.
- Trained a YOLOv7 prototype to practice the basic object-detection workflow.
- The project was not productionized and its repository is intentionally not promoted.
